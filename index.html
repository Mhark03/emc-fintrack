BS Information System Research Proposal

EMC-FINTRACK: A Web-Based Financial Transparency System for Student Organizations on EMC Campus

In Partial Fulfillment of Requirements for Enterprise Architecture & Enterprise System with Feasibility Study

Researcher: MARK ANTHONY A. ACUZAR
Institution: Eastern Mindoro College, Inc.
Date of Presentation: October 25, 2026

1. Conceptual Framework (IPO Model)

Visual representation of Inputs, Development Processes, and Final System Outputs

INPUT
Respondent Profiles:

Year level, Course/Dept, Org Membership.

Functional Requirements:

Transaction & Receipt Uploading, Real-time Dashboard, Role-based Security.

PROCESS
System Requirements Analysis
System Architecture & UI/UX Design
Full-Stack Development (Frontend & DB)
Deployment & Usability Testing
OUTPUT
EMC-FinTrack Web Portal:

Digital receipt ledger, budget dashboard, role access, and audit capabilities.

2. Theoretical Foundations

DeLone & McLean IS Success Model: Evaluates System Quality, Information Quality, and Service Quality to ensure user adoption and trust among student leaders and advisers.

Agency Theory & Accountability Framework: Addresses principal-agent relationships by eliminating information asymmetry between student body officers (agents) and students/advisers (principals).

3. Scope & Delimitation

Scope: Covers student organization officers, general members, and advisers across EMCI departments. Focuses on digital uploads, ledger tracking, and balance computation.

Delimitation: Strictly delimited to financial tracking & auditing. Does not process live payment gateways (GCash, online banking) or institutional tuition funds.
